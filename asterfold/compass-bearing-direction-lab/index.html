Example: 0° and 360° are north, 90° is east, 180° is south and 270° is west. In this eight-direction model, 292° is still in the west sector; 292.5° is the boundary toward northwest.

Read clockwise from north

A bearing measures the turn from north, not the angle above the horizon. Altitude is a separate measurement.

Use the same north reference

True north and magnetic north are different references. Check which one your source and compass use before comparing a real-world reading.

A direction is not a visibility promise

Terrain, buildings and trees can block a view. The dial shows only a direction, and the Sun's real sunset bearing depends on location and date.

Before you use the result

Why does a whole range say west?

An eight-point compass groups bearings into 45-degree sectors. West covers 247.5° up to, but not including, 292.5° in this tool.

Does the Sun always set at 270°?

No. The sunset bearing varies with place and date. This practice dial does not calculate it.

Will this point my phone toward the Sun?

No. The arrow is an illustration controlled by your input; it does not read your device orientation.
