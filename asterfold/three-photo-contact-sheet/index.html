Camera-club exercise / local contact sheet

Three frames.
One visual story.

Put a wide view, a clear subject and a small detail side by side. Try a new order. Let the pictures start the conversation.

The starter images are composition illustrations, not real photographs. Replace any frame with your own JPEG, PNG or WebP, up to 15 MB each. HEIC is not supported; save a JPEG copy first.

Selected photos and captions stay in this open browser tab. This tool does not upload or save them. Reloading clears them. Ordinary page requests still reach the hosting provider.

    A conversation, not a score

    Look. Compare. Revisit.

    1. Look: Which frame makes the place easiest to understand? Name one visible clue.
    2. Compare: What changes when the detail comes first instead of last? Try that order.
    3. Revisit: Choose one framing decision to try on the next photo walk.

    Run a ten-minute club exercise.

    Work alone or in pairs. Spend three minutes selecting frames, three minutes comparing two orders, and four minutes describing a choice. Keep feedback about the pictures; no rankings are needed.

    Use only photos you own or have permission to share. Clubs and educators may reuse these original instructions and starter illustrations under CC BY 4.0. The license does not apply to visitors’ photos. No app purchase or endorsement is required.
