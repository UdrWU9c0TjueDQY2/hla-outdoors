Independent companion activity · Asterfold: Sunset Planner

Three labels for an honest sunset log

Independent field-note exercise: divide a page into 'planned,' 'forecast' and 'observed.' Use a fictional outing. Under planned, write 'photograph the pier silhouette.' Under forecast, write 'cloud description to check before leaving.' Under observed, leave a blank until the outing actually occurs. Do not copy the forecast into the observation column. After a real visit, add only what was seen and the date, without exposing a private location publicly. The finished page separates intention, prediction and evidence. Asterfold provides native viewpoint planning and a journal for a similar recurring habit, while weather and actual visibility remain uncertain.
