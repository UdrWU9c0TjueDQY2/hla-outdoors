MAKE THE RESULT USEFUL

Arrival time is not ready time.

With sunset at 16:45, a 20-minute lead, 10 minutes of setup and 35 minutes of travel, leave at 15:40. Arrive at 16:15 and be ready at 16:25.

  • Count parking and the walk to the viewpoint in your travel estimate.
  • Allow your own setup margin for equipment and composition.
  • A hill, building or tree may hide the Sun before the listed sunset time.
